The two forms

On the server, a companion is either anchored, an entity held by its chunk, or an anchor, a player of the world that holds the world loaded around it.

Why two: a companion must be as cheap as a villager when nobody needs it, and load the world exactly like a player when its owner does. One vanilla class cannot be both.

FactRead in the 26.3 code: a world registers as one of its players any ServerPlayer it starts tracking (ServerLevel), and the chunk map loads chunks around it (ChunkMap.addEntity). A player is never stored with a chunk.

When a companion takes each form

DecisionA companion takes the anchor form when its owner puts it in follow mode, and the anchored form otherwise. The other modes are not designed yet. A player has at most one companion in follow mode at a time.
DecisionWhen its owner disconnects, the companion leaves the world with them, saved by the mod, and comes back where it was when they connect again.
  1. A companion starts anchored, wherever it comes from.
  2. Its owner puts it in follow mode: it becomes a player of the world, and loads the world as it goes.
  3. Another mode, or it loses its owner: it goes back to being an ordinary entity, in the chunk where it stands.
  4. Its owner disconnects: the companion leaves the world with them, saved by the mod.
  5. Its owner connects again: the companion comes back, anchor, where it was.

What the anchor form keeps of a player

DecisionEvery effect of a player, on purpose. It counts for sleeping, so an owner who wants to skip the night places a bed for their companion too; it makes monsters spawn around it.

The names

DecisionAnchored and anchor. The anchored form is held by its chunk; the anchor form holds the world loaded around it. The names say what the form is to the world, not what it does for its owner: another mode than follow may take the anchor form later.
