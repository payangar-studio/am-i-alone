# Where companions live

Not designed yet.

## In game

Companions appear without owner in the world: in village taverns, and sometimes
in structures. A player meets them there and can make one their own.

## How it is built

Not designed. What is known: an unowned companion is in the anchored form, an
entity saved with its chunk (`../companion/01-forms.md`), so a structure can
hold several at the cost of villagers.
