Am I Alone?

Am I Alone?

am_i_alone · both-required · Fabric · Minecraft 26.3

The mod adds companions: characters that look and move like players, but that nobody controls. Some live in village taverns or structures; a player can make one their own, the way one tames a wolf, and give it orders.

Features

  1. Companion: the character itself, which every other feature acts on. Designed; the first to build.
  2. Ownership and modes: a player owns a companion and gives it orders, such as follow. Partly decided.
  3. Behaviour: what a companion does on its own, according to its mode. Not designed.
  4. Where companions live: taverns and structures where companions appear without owner. Not designed.
  5. Companion debug stick: scaffolding, an operator's item to test what has no interaction in the game yet, such as opening a companion's inventory. Designed with the inventory.

Pages for the whole mod: Sides, what runs on the client and on the server, and what crosses between them.

Glossary

WordMeaning
CompanionA character of the mod, owned or not: one ordinary entity on the server, with a name, a skin, an owner, a mode, a home and a memory.
Companion ticketA chunk ticket of the mod's own type, which a companion places where it stands while it follows its owner: the world around it lives as around a player.
FacadeWhat a client sees of a companion: a remote player, which the player animation mods animate.
OwnerThe player a companion belongs to. A companion may have none.
ModeThe order an owner gives their companion. Only follow is decided so far.
HomeThe place a companion stays around when its mode says so.
Designated weaponOne of a companion's two weapon slots, primary and secondary: what it reaches for to fight, not what it holds.
Companion debug stickThe operators' scaffolding item that tests what has no interaction in the game yet. Vanilla's own is always called vanilla's debug stick.
Connected playerA player of the server's list (PlayerList): counted, subject to the connection limit, shown in Tab. A companion never is one.
Player of the worldA player of a world's list (ServerLevel.players): loads chunks, makes monsters spawn, counts for sleeping.

Minecraft and Fabric terms

TermWhat it is
Player, ServerPlayerVanilla's class of every player, and its server version for a connected player.
RemotePlayerThe class a client uses for the other players it sees.
Mannequin, AvatarVanilla's player-shaped decoration entity, and Avatar, the class it shares with players: a player-shaped entity that is not a player.
BrainVanilla's AI system (villagers, piglins, warden): memories, sensors, activities.
Global attachmentData that Fabric API keeps for the whole server and saves with the world.
Synced attachmentData that Fabric API attaches to an entity and sends to the clients that see it.
mod.tomlThis project's description of the mod, from which the build writes fabric.mod.json.

Better CombatAnimates it as a player, but a held weapon's pose and the attacks need an adapter; optionalClient facade
Ragdoll Deaths, Simple BloodChange how its death looksDeath
Tools on my BackShows a player's tools on their back; not checked on a companionInventory
Death LocatorMarks where a player died; not checked on a companionDeath

Open questions

  • An issue to open on Ragdoll Deaths (Death).
  • Disconnection at server stop, and an owner who never comes back (Saving).
  • Taming and the other modes (Ownership and modes).
  • The motor, moving through a portal, hostile mobs and companions (Behaviour).
  • Where companions live (Where companions live).
