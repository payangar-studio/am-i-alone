Am I Alone?

am_i_alone · both-required · Fabric · Minecraft 26.3

The mod adds companions: characters that look and animate like players, but that nobody controls. Some live unowned in village taverns or structures; a player can make one their own, the way one tames a wolf, and give it orders: follow, decided so far, and later others such as stay or wander.

The whole

  1. The core is the companion: its identity (UUID, name, skin), owner, mode and home. See Shared identity.
  2. It lives in one of two forms: anchored when nobody needs it, anchor when it must load the world like a player. See The two forms and Switching forms.
  3. A client sees one facade, a remote player, which never changes when the form does. See Sides.
  4. Because the facade is a player on the client, the player animation mods animate it.

Requirements

RequirementWhat it means
1. Animated like a playerOwned or not, the player animation mods (EMF, ETF, Fresh Animations Player Extension, Not Enough Animations, Touch'n Motion, Better Combat, EMF Compat) animate it with no compatibility code of ours.
2. Cheap when unownedA tavern may hold several: they cost like villagers and freeze with their chunk.
3. Like a player when it followsEvery effect of a player: chunks, monster spawning, sleeping.
4. Never a connected playerNot counted, takes no slot of the connection limit, not shown in Tab.
5. Nothing lost, nothing duplicatedWhatever happens (switching form, its owner leaving, the server stopping), it keeps exactly its inventory, its equipment and everything else.
6. An invisible switchChanging form does not show in game.
7. No mixinWhile a vanilla or Fabric API does the job. The invisible switch needs one, on the client.

Glossary

WordMeaning
CompanionA character of the mod, owned or not. Its identity, owner, mode, home and memory outlive its changes of form.
CoreWhat the companion is, carried by its current form: identity, owner, mode, home, and the rule that picks the form.
FormHow a companion exists in the world on the server. It always has exactly one.
Anchored formLives as an ordinary entity, held by its chunk: saved with it, frozen when it unloads.
Anchor formA player of the world: holds the world loaded around it, like a player ("anchor" is the usual name of a chunk loader in mods).
FacadeWhat a client sees of a companion: one remote player, the same object whatever the form behind it.
OwnerThe player a companion belongs to. A companion may have none.
ModeThe order an owner gives their companion. Only follow is decided so far.
HomeThe place a companion stays around when its mode says so.
Connected playerA player of the server's list (PlayerList): counted, subject to the connection limit, shown in Tab. A companion never is one.
Player of the worldA player of a world's list (ServerLevel.players): loads chunks, makes monsters spawn, counts for sleeping.

Minecraft and Fabric terms used here

TermWhat it is
Player, ServerPlayerVanilla's class of every player, and its server version for a connected player.
RemotePlayerThe class a client uses for the other players it sees.
Mannequin, AvatarVanilla's player-shaped decoration entity, and the class it shares with players.
makeMockPlayerVanilla's helper that creates a player without a connection, for its own tests.
BrainVanilla's AI system (villagers, piglins, warden): memories, sensors, activities.
Global attachmentData that Fabric API keeps for the whole server and saves with the world.
Synced attachmentData that Fabric API attaches to an entity and sends to the clients that see it.
