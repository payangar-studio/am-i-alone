Saving

A companion is saved with its chunk like any entity; a following companion leaves with its owner and comes back with them.

In game

A companion survives everything a world goes through: a restart, a crash, its owner leaving. A following companion leaves with its owner when they disconnect, and comes back with them, where it was.

How it is built

DecisionA companion in the world is an ordinary entity, saved with its chunk by vanilla. Its ticket is never saved: it is placed again when needed (World loading).
DecisionThe mod's data keeps, for each owner, their following companion: its UUID, dimension and position while it is in the world, and its whole entity save while it is away with its owner. Never in the players' files.

DecisionIts inventory is part of its entity save (Inventory), so it goes wherever the companion goes, with nothing more to write.

Step by step

  1. The owner disconnects (ServerPlayConnectionEvents.DISCONNECT): the companion's entity save is written to its owner's entry, its ticket is removed, and the companion leaves the world, discarded so that its chunk forgets it.
  2. A world save: vanilla writes the chunks, with the companions in the world, and the mod's data, in the same save.
  3. The owner connects (ServerPlayConnectionEvents.JOIN): the mod reads their entry, loads that chunk with a ticket as vanilla does for a joining player (TicketType.PLAYER_SPAWN), recreates the companion from its save if it was away (or finds it in its chunk), and places its ticket again.
DecisionAn entry's saved entity is removed when the companion comes back; the whole entry when it stops following, dies (Death) or is removed for good. An entry left behind would bring a second companion back.

A crash between two saves brings the chunks and the mod's data back to the same moment, since they are written in the same save. A crash while the save is being written is not covered; vanilla has the same window between the players' files and the chunks.

QuestionDisconnection at server stop. Whether vanilla runs the disconnection of every player when the server stops, to check. If it does not, the companion stays saved with its chunk where it was, and the owner's next connection finds it there through the position kept in the entry.
QuestionAn owner who never comes back. Their companion stays in the mod's data, out of the world, for good; whether it should come back after a while, without owner, is not decided.
