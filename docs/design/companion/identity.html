Identity and skins

A companion's name and skin, decided once and shown on every client.

In game

A companion has a name and a skin. Its skin is one of the skins the mod ships, chosen when it is created, and it never changes after, like a wolf's variant.

How it is built

DecisionThe identity is the companion's UUID (its entity UUID), its name and its skin variant, saved with the entity. The name and the skin variant reach the clients as a Fabric synced attachment of the entity.
  1. The companion holds its name and its skin variant, saved with it.
  2. They travel as a Fabric synced attachment of the entity.
  3. Fabric sends it to every client that sees the entity; the facade takes its name and skin from it.
FactThey cannot be synched data of the companion: any data it declared would take id 17 or more (ClassTreeIdRegistry.java:32), which the client's ClientCompanion uses for Player's own data (Player.java:135-140); a serializer that does not match makes the client fail (SynchedEntityData.java:119).

Skins as variants

DecisionThe skin is a variant, chosen once when the companion is created and never changed: the companion holds a reference to one entry of the mod's skins, each entry a texture (assets/am_i_alone/textures/entity/companion/<id>.png) and a model, wide or slim. The client builds its skin from it (ClientCompanion.getSkin, Client facade).
FactRead in the 26.3 code: vanilla's entity variants (pig_variant, wolf_variant...) are registries of entries, chosen at spawn (VariantUtils.selectVariantToSpawn) and saved with the entity.
DecisionThe mod's skins behave as vanilla's variants: a registry filled by data (am_i_alone:companion_skin), so a datapack or another mod can add skins, and the server sends the list to the clients.
DecisionRuled out: a player's skin, which comes from Mojang's servers with a signed profile, needs the network and ties a companion to an account.
