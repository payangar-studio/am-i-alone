Companion

The character itself, the first feature to build. Owner and modes come later (Ownership and modes).

In game

A companion looks, moves and animates like a player: the player animation mods (Fresh Animations, Not Enough Animations, Touch'n Motion, Better Combat...) animate it as they animate players. It is not a player: it takes no slot on the server, and it is not in the Tab list.

When nobody needs it, it lives like a villager: it stays where it is, and freezes when its area unloads. When it follows its owner, it keeps the world alive around it like a player. Whatever happens, it keeps everything it has.

DecisionA companion is not a player: it gains a player's abilities one at a time, each chosen on purpose (keeping the world alive around it, a death message like a player's...), rather than behaving as a player in everything.
DecisionIts skin is one of the skins the mod ships, like an entity's variant: chosen when the companion is created, it never changes after. Players' own skins are not used.
DecisionA companion that dies is dead for good, like a creature (Death).

Requirements

RequirementWhat it means
1. Animated like a playerOwned or not, by the player animation mods (EMF, ETF, Fresh Animations Player Extension, Not Enough Animations, Touch'n Motion, Better Combat, EMF Compat).
2. Cheap when nobody needs itA tavern may hold several: they cost like villagers and freeze with their area.
3. Keeps the world alive when it follows its ownerThe world around it lives as around a player: chunks loaded, entities, machines, crops growing.
4. Not a player of the serverNot in the Tab list, not counted among the players, never in the way of a player who wants to join.
5. Nothing lost, nothing duplicatedWhatever happens to it, its owner leaving or the server stopping, it keeps everything it has.

How it is built

DecisionA companion is not a player. It gets a player's abilities one by one, each through a vanilla mechanism the mod drives itself (its own chunk ticket, the death message vanilla builds, broadcast with vanilla's functions...), and never by taking a player's behaviour as a whole.

A companion is one ordinary entity on the server, which clients build as a player. When it follows its owner, it places a chunk ticket of the mod's own type where it stands, and the world around it lives as around a player.

  1. The companion is one ordinary entity on the server, never a player, saved with its chunk. See The entity.
  2. When it follows its owner, it places the companion ticket, a chunk ticket of the mod's own type, where it stands, and moves it with it: the world around it lives as around a player. See World loading.
  3. A client builds the companion as a player, its facade. See Client facade.
  4. The facade is a player on the client, so the player animation mods animate it.

Classes

ClassWhat it is
CompanionThe companion on the server: an entity extending Avatar, vanilla's player-shaped entity that is not a player (the Mannequin's class), saved with its chunk
ClientCompanionThe facade on the client, extending RemotePlayer: what the client builds for a companion, so the animation mods see a player
Companion ticketNot a class: a TicketType of the mod, registered like vanilla's, which a following companion places and moves with it
DecisionNo mixin. Every piece goes through a vanilla or Fabric API. One may come later for despawning around a companion far from any player (World loading).
DecisionUntil the other features exist, commands stand in for them: vanilla's /summon am_i_alone:companion creates a companion (where the places will), and a test command will place or remove its ticket (where follow mode will), its syntax decided with the issue of the loading brick.

Ruled out

OptionWhy it is ruled out
A server entity extending Player, in two forms (an entity at rest, a ServerPlayer when following) switched behind one client entityBeing a Player on the server brought one problem after another: its UUID forced back from its profile on every load, a scoreboard name taken from its name and colliding with a real player of the same name, a player's death path, an overridden entity type with a hidden coupling, a form switch that needed a client mixin and a handover of everything, two places to save it
A hidden fake ServerPlayer, an anchor, standing next to the companion to load the worldTwo entities for one: it had to be hidden from clients, from mobs, from collisions, from the locator bar, from experience orbs and from /kill, and it still did not bring natural spawning or despawning around the companion
A token ServerPlayer, never in the world, registered in the distance manager (DistanceManager.addPlayer)The same chunk result as a ticket, with more risk: a player object that only exists as a key, statistics and advancements registered for it, a raised global mob cap
One fake connected player for every companion (like Carpet's bots)Counted among the players, limited by max-players, shown in Tab unless worked around; a player's cost for each companion, even unowned; never stored with its chunk
