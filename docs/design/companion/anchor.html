The anchor

What keeps the world alive around a companion that follows its owner, without anything else showing.

In game

When a companion follows its owner, the world stays alive around it exactly as around a player: the area around it stays loaded, monsters spawn there and mobs do not despawn, and it counts for sleeping, so its owner places a bed for it too to skip the night.

Nothing else shows: nobody sees, touches, pushes or attacks what does it.

How it is built

DecisionA following companion has an anchor, CompanionAnchor, a hidden ServerPlayer that stands at the companion's position, moved there every tick, and does only what a player does to the world around it. It has no identity, no inventory and no rendering of its own.
DecisionThe anchor is added to the world (level.addNewPlayer) but never to the server's list of connected players: a player of the world, not a connected player. Its fake connection drops everything sent to it: a disconnected connection would queue packets without limit (Connection.java:283).

Lifecycle

  1. The companion starts following (or its owner connects while it follows): it creates its anchor.
  2. Every tick, the anchor is moved to the companion's position.
  3. The companion stops following, its owner disconnects, or it dies: the anchor is removed.
  4. Something kills the anchor (/kill @e reaches it): it is recreated at once.
DecisionUntil ownership is built, a test command attaches or detaches the anchor, and records the player who runs it as the companion's owner, so that leaving and coming back with the owner work as designed (Saving).
DecisionA stable UUID per anchor, different from its companion's. Two entities sharing a UUID would crash addNewPlayer, which casts the existing entity to a ServerPlayer (ServerLevel.java:987-991).

Sleeping

FactA non-spectator anchor counts as an active player for sleeping (SleepStatus.java:39-45): with one real player and a 100% threshold, two sleepers are needed. Vanilla's sleeping needs a real bed (LivingEntity.java:3773) and the body tick the anchor never runs (ServerPlayer.java:581-620).
DecisionWhen its companion sleeps in a bed, the anchor is marked asleep (setSleepingPos, isSleepingLongEnough answering true) and the world's sleeping count is updated (updateSleepingPlayerList). How a companion goes to bed is designed with its behaviour (Behaviour).

Known side effects

FactStats and advancements: a ServerPlayer registers both in the player list's maps when it is built (ServerPlayer.java:378-379); they are not saved for a player outside the list (PlayerList.java:622), but they stay in the maps, hence a stable UUID per anchor. Advancement announcements go through the player list (PlayerAdvancements.java:179).
FactAccepted as player-like: the wandering trader and patrols may pick the anchor as the player they spawn near (WanderingTraderSpawner.java:76, PatrolSpawner.java:31); monster spawners count it (EntityGetter.java:100).
QuestionExperience orbs fly toward the anchor and are never collected (ExperienceOrb.java:157). To solve without a mixin if possible.
