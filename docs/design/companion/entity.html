The entity

Technical page: what a companion is on the server

In game

A companion is a character of the world like a villager: it is saved where it stands, freezes when its area unloads, and dies like a creature.

How it is built

DecisionOn the server, a companion is one entity, Companion, extending Avatar: vanilla's player-shaped entity that is not a player, the class the Mannequin and every player share. A companion is never a Player on the server, and never changes class.

Why Avatar: what makes the animation is on the client (Client facade); the server only needs an entity whose data a client player can read. Everything a Player would bring on the server (a profile, a player's scoreboard name, a player's death, a connection) is what the companion must not have.

  1. Avatar is vanilla's player-shaped entity that is not a player: the Mannequin's class, and the parent of Player (Player.java:126).
  2. On the server, the companion extends Avatar directly and declares no data of its own.
  3. On the client, the factory of the companion type builds a ClientCompanion, a player: see Client facade.
DecisionThe companion has its own entity type, am_i_alone:companion, declared as EntityType<Avatar>: its factory returns a Companion on the server and a ClientCompanion on the client, two different subclasses of Avatar.
DecisionThe companion declares no synched data of its own: its identity and skin travel as a Fabric synced attachment (Identity, names and skins).
DecisionThe companion sets the model customisation byte of Avatar (the skin layers shown: hat, jacket, sleeves, trousers), as the Mannequin does (Mannequin.java:61); its default, 0, would hide them (AvatarRenderer.java:164-170).
