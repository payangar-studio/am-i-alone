World loading

A following companion keeps the world alive around it with a chunk ticket of the mod's own type, the way vanilla does for a player.

In game

When a companion follows its owner, the world around it lives exactly as around a player: chunks stay loaded, entities move, machines run, crops grow. When nobody needs it, it freezes with its area like any creature.

How it is built

FactRead in the 26.3 code: a player keeps the world alive only through tickets. DistanceManager.addPlayer places a PLAYER_SIMULATION ticket (simulate and keep-dimension-active flags) at level 31 minus the simulation distance, and PLAYER_LOADING tickets (loading flag) on the chunks within view distance (DistanceManager.java:109-133, 267-283).
FactEverything that makes a chunk live depends on tickets only, never on a player being there:
What lives in a chunkDecided byReference (26.3)
Entities move and actThe chunk's ticket level: entity tickingServerLevel.java:438
Scheduled block and fluid ticks (redstone, water...)The chunk's ticket level: block tickingServerLevel.java:478

DecisionThe mod registers its own TicketType (its constructor new TicketType(0L, flags) is public; registered in BuiltInRegistries.TICKET_TYPE like vanilla's) with the loading, simulation and keep-dimension-active flags. A following companion places it at its chunk, with a player's radius (the simulation distance plus 2; the view distance for loading if it is a separate ticket), and moves it each time it changes chunk.
  1. The ticket is placed when follow mode starts, or when the owner of a following companion connects.
  2. It moves each time the companion changes chunk.
  3. It is removed when follow mode ends, when the owner disconnects (the companion leaves with them), or when the companion dies.
FactInferred, not tested: a ticket asks for its whole area at once, where a player's loading tickets are spread over time chunk by chunk.

What a ticket does not bring

FactRead in the 26.3 code: some things only happen near a real player, whatever the tickets.

DecisionIn follow mode they come from the owner, who is near the companion by definition; when the owner disconnects, the companion leaves with them, ticket included. Nothing is added for them now.
QuestionA future mode that keeps the world alive far from any player would need despawning (and maybe spawning) around the companion. Vanilla has no API for it. Lead, not checked in detail: one targeted mixin in Mob.checkDespawn, where it looks up the nearest player; spawning would need several injection points.
