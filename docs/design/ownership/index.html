Ownership and modes

Partly decided, not built yet. Its first brick is recruitment: a player pays a companion without an owner in emeralds, and owns it.

In game

A player can make a companion their own by recruiting it, and give it an order, its mode.

  1. A player recruits a companion that has no owner by paying its price in emeralds on its recruitment screen (Recruitment). Then it is theirs, and that is all: it does not follow them, it has no mode yet.
  2. Follow, the only mode decided: the companion goes with its owner, into another dimension too.
DecisionRecruitment: a player makes a companion that has no owner their own by paying its price in emeralds, on its recruitment screen (Recruitment). A companion that has an owner cannot be recruited.
DecisionFollow: the companion goes with its owner, into another dimension too. It is the only mode decided. Whether a companion follows its owner through a portal depends on its mode.
DecisionOne companion in follow mode per player at a time.
DecisionIt leaves with its owner: when the owner disconnects, a following companion leaves the world with them, and comes back where it was when they connect again.
DecisionNot counted for sleeping: a following companion keeps the world alive around it, but it is not a player, so it does not count among the sleepers needed to skip the night. (An earlier decision, that it counted and needed its own bed, is withdrawn.)
QuestionOther conditions to recruit a companion; a price that grows with its equipment; an owner giving their companion to another player; the other modes (stay, wander, stay around its home), which will come later.

How it is built

The owner and the mode are saved with the companion; the owner is a synced attachment, set by recruitment (Recruitment). Follow mode is what makes the companion place its ticket (World loading); leaving and coming back with the owner is how a following companion is saved (Saving).
